What to notice. Two windows slide along with today, and they guard different things. The 24-month flight review decides whether you may fly as pilot in command at all; the 90-day window of 3 take-offs and 3 landings decides only whether you may take a passenger, so a lapse there is fixed by a few solo circuits. Your passenger currency runs out 90 days after your third most recent landing, however many you did before it. Figures from CASR 61.395 and 61.400 as the lessons give them; verify the current text.

Try it: Priya, today 3 October. What does she need, and in what order?

Press Priya: her review of 18 August 2023 ran to 31 August 2025, so it has expired and she may fly only dual. Only the 24 August and 20 September flights are inside the 90 days: 2 of 3. A flight review with an instructor comes first, and its landings count towards the 3.

Try it: Alex has only 2 landings in the window. What do the solo circuits on Saturday 4 October buy him?

Press Alex, then Alex after circuits: two more landings make 4 in the window, so he may take his friends on Sunday, and his passenger currency now lasts until 90 days after his third most recent landing.
