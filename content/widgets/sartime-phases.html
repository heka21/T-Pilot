What happens

What to notice. Nothing at all happens until the SARTIME passes; after that the system climbs through the uncertainty, alert and distress phases, from calls to you, to a communications search, to a physical search along your notified route, with the effort growing only as the evidence grows. The flight times are the lesson's Hyden example; the lessons give the order of the phases, not their timings, so the phases here are drawn in order and not to scale.

Try it: on the delayed flight, what would have happened if you had not called Flight Information?

Choose "Delayed" and step to Extend by radio: without the call the SARTIME stays at 0350 UTC (1150 WST), and you land at 1145 with five minutes to cancel. Miss it and the uncertainty phase starts for an aircraft that is on the ground. Extend before it expires, not after.

Try it: after the forced landing, why stay with the aircraft rather than walk to the nearest farm?

Play the forced landing to the distress phase: the physical search follows the route you notified. An aircraft on that route is far easier to see from the air than a person on foot, and it gives you shade.
