Pressure height (1013 set)

What to notice. Each hectopascal of difference between the subscale and the real QNH moves the reading about 30 ft. Set too high a number (or fly from high pressure into low without resetting) and the altimeter over-reads: you are lower than it says. With 1013 set it shows pressure height, $\text{altitude} + (1013 - \text{QNH}) \times 30$.

Try this: the aerodrome is at 1,200 ft and QNH is 1008. What is the pressure height?

$1200 + (1013 - 1008) \times 30 = 1200 + 150 = 1350$ ft. Press "Same place, 1013 set": the altimeter now shows 1,350 ft.

Try this: you leave 1023 set when the QNH is 1013 and hold 1,500 ft on the altimeter. Where are you really?

The altimeter over-reads by $(1023 - 1013) \times 30 = 300$ ft, so you are at 1,200 ft: 300 ft lower than shown. High to low, look out below.
