What to notice. Dead reckoning is the last fix plus groundspeed × time along the track made good, using the latest measured TMG and groundspeed, not the planned ones. Its error grows the longer you go without a fix, so the area to search keeps widening (the circle's size is illustrative). DR tells you where to look; map reading confirms it. Plot it on the chart in true, after the variation.

Try it: last fix 1015 over a siding, TMG 075° M, groundspeed 105 kt. Where are you at 1029?

14 minutes at 1.75 nm a minute is 24.5 nm: 24.5 nm along 075° M from the siding. Search the features within a few miles of that point. Press DR at 1029.

Try it: why is six minutes a handy interval?

It is a tenth of an hour, so in 6 minutes you cover a tenth of your groundspeed: 10.5 nm at 105 kt. Press Six-minute rule.
