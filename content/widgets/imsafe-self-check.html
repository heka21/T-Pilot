Honest noes
Slices off
Decision

What to notice. IMSAFE forces a separate, honest answer to each question. One no cancels the flight on its own; several "not too bad" answers take a slice each and add up. The decision is yours as pilot in command, every day, whatever your medical certificate says. How big a slice each factor takes is illustrative; the questions and the rules are the lesson's.

Try it: you wake with a blocked nose before a solo navigation exercise and think a decongestant will get you through. Go or no go?

Press Blocked nose: Illness is a no on its own (the ears and sinuses block on the descent), and a decongestant you have not cleared with a DAME is a second no that may wear off in flight. Cancel and rebook.

Try it: two beers finished at 2100, a bad night, no breakfast, a headache and a work worry before an 1100 solo to Northam. What now?

Press Northam Saturday: the alcohol is fine, but four slices are off. Press After water and a meal: the headache has gone and the worry is put away, leaving one slice. If you are clear-headed by the briefing, fly; if not, rebook.
