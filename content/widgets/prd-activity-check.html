What to notice. The chart only tells you where and how high; ERSA gives the standing hours and the controlling authority, and a NOTAM can switch an area on or off, so the bottom row is the answer only after all three. ERSA and NOTAM times are UTC: add 8 hours for WST. Prohibited means never, restricted means only with a clearance when active, danger means you may enter but the risk is yours to know. The lesson gives no sunrise or sunset times: for HJ, set them yourself.

Try it: on the Saturday example, from what time WST could you route through without a clearance?

The NOTAM activates the area 0100 to 0800 UTC, which is 0900 to 1600 WST. Drag your time to 1600 WST: the activation has ended and the "by NOTAM" entry means it is inactive again, so you may route through (and before 0900 WST too).

Try it: the restricted area is active at your time. What changes if the controlling authority gives you a clearance?

Tick the clearance: a restricted area is treated like controlled airspace, so with the controlling authority's clearance you may enter while it is active. A prohibited area offers no such option, and a danger area needs no clearance at all.
