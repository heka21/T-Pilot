What to notice. When the alternator stops nothing goes dark at first: the battery takes over, and the only signs are the ammeter on discharge, sagging volts and a warning light. From then on the battery is a clock, and every service you leave on runs it down faster; the magnetos are not on this system, so the engine never notices. The minutes are illustrative, set to the lessons' "perhaps 30 to 60 minutes".

Try it: the alternator fails 40 minutes from home with everything on. What do you do?

Press Alternator fails, then Everything left on: at this load the battery is flat in about half an hour, before you get home. Check the alternator switch and field breaker and cycle it once if the POH allows; if it stays off, press Shed load (pitot heat, lights and the fuel pump off unless you need them) and the battery lasts about twice as long. Land before it runs flat (BAKC 3.4).

Try it: the battery is flat. Will the engine stop?

No. Play to Battery flat: radios, transponder, lights, flaps and the turn coordinator are gone, but the magnetos make their own sparks from the engine's rotation. The ASI, altimeter and VSI need no power either.
