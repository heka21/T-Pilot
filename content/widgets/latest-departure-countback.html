What to notice. Count backwards from last light at the final landing point: minus 10 minutes, minus the flight time (rounded up, because for a deadline part of a minute counts as a whole one), minus every holding the TAFs make you carry fuel for, minus the ground time at each stop; then subtract 8 hours for the UTC on the form. Holding costs daylight as well as fuel. The aeroplane, legs and times are the lesson's fictional worked plan.

Try it: in the worked plan, why did the INTER at Northam move the latest departure 30 minutes earlier?

Untick the INTER: the clock drops to 157 min and the latest departure moves to 1610 WST. Tick it again and it is back to 1540. The holding fuel is there so that you can actually hold for up to 30 minutes, and if you do, you reach Jandakot 30 minutes later, so the backwards count has to include it.

Try it: a Jandakot TEMPO appears and you have finished briefing at 1500. Can you still go?

Press Briefed at 1500: the latest departure is now 1440 WST (0640 UTC), so leaving at 1500 lands you at 1907, 20 minutes past the latest arrival and after last light. The plan as drawn is dead. Leaving out Northam recovers about 70 minutes (40 on the ground and 30 of holding), but the legs change too, so redo the flight times and the count before you decide; or go another day.
