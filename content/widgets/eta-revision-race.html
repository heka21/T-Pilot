What to notice. Until you reach a fix you cannot see that the wind is costing you time. Two timed fixes give the real groundspeed, and the rest of the leg takes time in the same ratio: time to go = distance to go × time taken ÷ distance flown. Groundspeed is per hour, so a distance over minutes needs × 60. Revise the fuel and the SARTIME with the ETA.

Try it: the first 30 nm of a 75 nm leg took 18 minutes (planned 16). The fix was at 0942 WST. Revised ETA?

45 × 18 ÷ 30 = 27 min (the plan was 24). 0942 + 27 = 1009 WST, 3 minutes later than planned for that part of the leg. Check: 30 ÷ 18 × 60 = 100 kt, and 45 nm at 100 kt is 27 min.

Try it: the first 45 nm took 25 minutes against a plan of 23, fix at 1043 WST, 90 nm to go. ETA and groundspeed?

90 × 25 ÷ 45 = 50 min, so 1133 WST, 4 minutes later than planned for that stretch. Groundspeed 45 ÷ 25 × 60 = 108 kt. Press 45 of 135 nm in 25 min.
