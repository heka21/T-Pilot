Group
Meaning
Local time (WST = UTC + 8)

What to notice. Every group says one thing, always in the same order: where, when, wind, visibility, weather, cloud, then the change groups. Times are UTC, so add 8 hours for WST and watch the date tick over; winds are from, in degrees true; cloud heights are above the aerodrome, and only BKN or OVC makes a ceiling. The spells drawn inside a TEMPO or INTER period show their length, not when they will come.

Try it: a change group reads FM031000. At what local time does the change happen?

From the 3rd at 1000 UTC; add 8 hours: 1800 WST on the 3rd, early evening in Perth. Press FM to see it on the time strip.

Try it: you plan to arrive at Jandakot at 1530 WST on the 3rd. Which part of the TAF do you plan for?

1530 WST is 0730 UTC, inside TEMPO 0306/0309: plan for 4,000 m in showers with a ceiling at 2,000 ft, below the 5,000 m VMC visibility. The SPECI at 0715Z (1515 WST) shows exactly that spell arriving.
