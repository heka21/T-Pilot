Holding fuel

What to notice. The first line of a TAF is not the forecast for your arrival: every change group that covers your time counts, and you cannot plan to arrive between spells. For a PPL flight the test runs from 30 minutes before to 60 minutes after the ETA, and how long the bad weather lasts sets the holding fuel: INTER 30 minutes, TEMPO 60, a permanent change an alternate.

Try it: on the Jandakot TAF of the 3rd, when is the earliest you can plan to arrive in the main conditions?

Drag the ETA past the end of the TEMPO at 0900 UTC (1700 WST): from then the main conditions apply, and from 1000 UTC (1800 WST) CAVOK.

Try it: Esperance, ETA 0630 UTC. The TEMPO starts at 0700, after you land. Why do you still need holding fuel?

Switch on the PPL window: it runs to 0730, so the TEMPO at 0700 is inside it. A TEMPO means 60 minutes of holding: 60 × 36 L/h = 36 L, or an alternate that meets the minima.
