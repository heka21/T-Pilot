What to notice. Crosswind $= V \sin\theta$ and headwind $= V \cos\theta$, where $\theta$ is the angle between the wind and the runway. The crosswind grows fast at first: 30° off already gives half the wind across, 60° gives nine-tenths, while the headwind is still half. Use the runway and wind in the same reference (both magnetic) before you subtract.

Try this: runway 24, wind 280/20. What are the components?

40° off from the right. Crosswind = 20 × sin 40° = 20 × 0.64 ≈ 13 kt; headwind = 20 × cos 40° = 20 × 0.77 ≈ 15 kt.

Try this: runway 06, wind 360/15, demonstrated crosswind 12 kt. Can you use it?

60° off from the left: crosswind = 15 × 0.87 ≈ 13 kt, over a 12 kt demonstrated figure; headwind only 15 × 0.5 ≈ 8 kt. Look for a more into-wind runway.
