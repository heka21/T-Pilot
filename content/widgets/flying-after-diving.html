What to notice. The extra nitrogen dissolved in the tissues at depth leaves over many hours, and climbing lowers the pressure that holds it in solution. Wait at least 12 hours after a dive without decompression stops and at least 24 hours after one with stops or after several days of diving; snorkelling needs no wait, and the wait applies whatever height you plan to fly. The waits are the lesson's (commonly taught; check current CASA guidance); the shape of the nitrogen curve is illustrative, not a dive table.

Try it: you surface from a no-stop dive off Rottnest at 1000 and want to fly back to Jandakot at 1,500 ft that afternoon. When can you go?

Press Rottnest, same afternoon: 1500 is 7 hours too soon, and a low cruise does not help. Press Rottnest, 2200: 1000 plus 12 hours is the earliest; realistically, fly the next day.

Try it: four days of diving at Ningaloo, the last dive ending at 1500 on Sunday. When is the earliest flight home?

Press Ningaloo week: several days of diving needs at least 24 hours even though no single dive needed stops, so Monday 1500 at the earliest.
