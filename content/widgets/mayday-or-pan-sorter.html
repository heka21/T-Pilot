What to notice. The test is not how frightening it feels but whether immediate assistance is needed. The same problem can be either call: lost with fuel to spare is a PAN PAN, lost with fuel nearly exhausted a MAYDAY; a rough engine still producing power is a PAN PAN, an engine failure a MAYDAY. A PAN can always be upgraded.

Try it: you are lost over the Darling Range with two hours of fuel. Distress or urgency, and what would change your answer?

Urgency: the situation concerns the safety of the aircraft, but with two hours of fuel the danger is not imminent, so a PAN PAN. If the fuel became nearly exhausted while you were still lost, the danger would become grave and imminent and you would upgrade to a MAYDAY.
