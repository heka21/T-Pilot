Listen on
Because that is where

What to notice. Test the radio before you move, then keep a continuous listening watch where whoever may need to reach you is transmitting: the CTAF within 10 nm of a non-controlled aerodrome, the ATC frequency in or near controlled airspace, the area frequency everywhere else, and 121.5 MHz on the second radio where you are able. The map is illustrative and not to scale; real frequencies come from ERSA and the charts.

Try it: at a non-controlled aerodrome with nobody on the CTAF, how do you know your receiver works before taxiing?

Listen to the AWIS (or ATIS) on its frequency: if you hear it clearly, the receiver works. A radio check on the CTAF proves your transmitter only if someone answers.

Try it: you depart Northam for Kellerberrin. When do you leave the Northam CTAF?

Step to Depart Northam: you stay on the CTAF until clear of the vicinity, then the area frequency until within 10 nm of Kellerberrin, then its CTAF.
