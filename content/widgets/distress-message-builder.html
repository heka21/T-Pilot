What to notice. The order is the thing to learn: signal, station, call-sign, nature, intentions, position, the rest. Who you are and what is wrong go out before where you are, and persons on board, endurance and colour come last because they help but are least essential. A PAN PAN uses exactly the same order.

Try it: five minutes after the York PAN PAN, the rough engine stops altogether. What do you do on the radio?

Upgrade to distress on the same frequency, after flying the aeroplane: “MAYDAY MAYDAY MAYDAY”, then station, call-sign, nature (engine failure), intentions (forced landing, at York if you can reach it), position, level and heading, and persons on board, in the standard order. Squawk 7700 if you have not already, and activate the ELT if the landing will be off-aerodrome.
