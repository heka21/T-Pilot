Verdict

What to notice. Below 10,000 ft the visibility is 5,000 m everywhere; what changes is the box you must keep out of around the cloud. Class C, Class E and Class G above 3,000 ft AMSL or 1,000 ft AGL (whichever is higher): 1,500 m and 1,000 ft. Class D is smaller, 600 m, 1,000 ft above and 500 ft below, because the tower keeps IFR traffic apart from the circuit. Low in Class G there is no box at all: clear of cloud and in sight of the ground or water. You need to be outside the box, so enough distance either sideways or vertically will do. Above 10,000 ft the visibility rises to 8 km. Figures from RFRC 2.3 and PFRA 2.6; confirm them against the current VFRG.

Try it: at 2,500 ft near Pinjarra, 1,000 m from a cloud. VMC in Class G? In Class E?

Press Pinjarra: at or below 3,000 ft AMSL in Class G you need only to be clear of cloud, so it is VMC. Press Same spot, Class E: the box is now 1,500 m wide, and 1,000 m puts you inside it. Not VMC.

Try it: in Class D you are 500 ft under a cloud base. Legal? What if the zone were Class C?

Press Class D 500 ft below: exactly on the minimum, so VMC. Switch the class to C and the box grows to 1,000 ft below the base: you are 500 ft inside it, and you would have to descend another 500 ft or move 1,500 m away sideways.
