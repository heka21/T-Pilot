What to notice. The squelch line belongs just above the hiss: any lower and you listen to static, any higher and the weak stations stop opening the gate, so a busy frequency sounds quiet. Open the squelch, set the volume against the hiss, then close it until the hiss just stops. The signal levels here are illustrative.

Try it: a busy frequency sounds strangely quiet, yet Jandakot Ground comes through clearly. What is the likely cause?

Choose Too tight: the strong nearby station still opens the gate, but the nearer and distant aircraft do not. Open the squelch until you hear hiss, then close it until the hiss just stops.

Try it: you hear nothing at all. Why open the squelch before touching the volume?

Choose Silent: stations are transmitting, but the volume is down, and that sounds exactly like a quiet frequency. Opening the squelch gives hiss, which proves the receiver and audio path work and gives you a sound to set the volume against.
