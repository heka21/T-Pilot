What to notice. Everything you sense has to queue for one channel of attention, and what waits too long in the sensory store simply fades. A passenger's chatter at the wrong moment and the speck of traffic never reaches your mental model; expectation can turn a call into the one you wanted to hear. Either way the decision is reasonable and wrong, because the picture it rests on is wrong. Timings are compressed and illustrative.
