If this link fails

What to notice. Your words go out through the microphone, PTT, transmitter and antenna, and the reply comes back through the same antenna, the receiver and the headset: transmitter and receiver share a box and an antenna but are separate paths. Every link does one job, so the job that is missing points you at the fault.

Try it: the ATIS on COM 2 is silent. Which links could be to blame, and which cannot?

Choose Listening to the ATIS: power, the COM 2 antenna, the COM 2 receiver or its settings, and the headset or audio routing. The microphone, PTT and transmitter play no part, because you are only receiving.

Try it: why is the avionics master switched on only after the engine has started?

Step back to Power: the starter’s heavy current causes voltage spikes and dips that can damage the solid-state radios. With the avionics bus off during the start, the transient never reaches them.
