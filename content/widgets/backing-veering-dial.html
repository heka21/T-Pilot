What to notice. Backing and veering describe only the movement round the dial: anticlockwise is backing, clockwise is veering, taken the shorter way round. Read it as movement, not as bigger or smaller numbers: 320 to 010 is a veer of 50° through north. The definitions are the same in both hemispheres; what differs is which one happens. At a Southern Hemisphere cold front the wind backs, north-west to south-west; at a Northern Hemisphere one it veers.

Try it: the Jandakot ATIS wind goes from 090 to 040 over an hour. Backed or veered, and by how much?

Press Jandakot ATIS: from east the wind moves anticlockwise towards north-east, so it has backed 50°.

Try it: the Busselton TAF has 34015G25KT, then FM 27020G32KT. What kind of change is that?

Press Busselton TAF: from 340 round to 270 is anticlockwise, a back of 70°, which in the Southern Hemisphere is the signature of a cold front passing.
