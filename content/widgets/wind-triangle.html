Groundspeed (triangle solved exactly)
Mental check: crosswind × 60 ÷ TAS, TAS − headwind

What to notice. The blue heading always points into the wind, upwind of the track: wind from the left gives right drift and a heading left of track. Solve the triangle in true (forecast winds are true), and only then apply the variation: west is best, east is least.

Try this: track 160°T, TAS 100 kt, wind 230/20. What heading and groundspeed?

The wind is 70° right of track: crosswind 20 × 0.94 = 18.8 kt, drift about 11° left, so steer 160 + 11 = 171°T. Headwind 20 × 0.34 = 6.8 kt; GS ≈ 100 × cos 11° − 6.8 ≈ 91 kt.

Try this: put the wind exactly on the nose. What happens to drift and groundspeed?

No crosswind, so no drift: heading and track are the same, and the groundspeed is the TAS minus the full wind speed.
