ceiling

What to notice. Oktas count how many eighths of the sky the cloud would fill if you pushed it all together: FEW 1 to 2, SCT 3 to 4, BKN 5 to 7, OVC 8. Only BKN and OVC make a ceiling, because then the sky is more cloud than gap, and the ceiling is the lowest such layer, which need not be the lowest cloud. Bases in a TAF or METAR are above the aerodrome. The cloud shapes are illustrative.

Try it: the Jandakot TAF opens with FEW030 SCT045. Is there a ceiling?

Press FEW030 SCT045: 2 oktas and 4 oktas, neither of them 5 or more, so no ceiling. Push the upper layer up by one okta to 5 and it becomes BKN045: now there is a ceiling at 4,500 ft.

Try it: a Northam METAR reads FEW020 BKN045. Where is the ceiling?

Press FEW020 BKN045: the ceiling is the broken layer at 4,500 ft above the aerodrome, not the few cloud at 2,000 ft. With Northam about 500 ft above sea level, that is 5,000 ft AMSL.
