What to notice. The take-off distance does not end when the wheels leave the ground: it runs from brake release to 50 ft at the take-off safety speed, at least 1.2 times the stall speed with take-off flap, and that whole distance must fit inside the TODA. The TODR, strip lengths and TOSS are the lesson's; the split between ground run and airborne segment and the lift-off speed are illustrative.

Try it: the POH stall speed with take-off flap is 47 kt. What is the least take-off safety speed?

1.2 × 47 = 56.4 kt, rounded up, never down: 57 kt. If the chart or POH gives a higher figure, fly that.

Try it: the chart gives 640 m. Why is a 600 m strip a no-go even though the aeroplane is airborne well before the end?

Choose the 600 m strip and play it: the aeroplane lifts off in time but crosses the end of the strip below 50 ft, so it is not yet clear of the fence and whatever lies beyond. Reduce weight until the chart gives a TODR of 600 m or less.
