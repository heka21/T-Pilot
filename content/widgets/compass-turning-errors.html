The rule

What to notice. In the southern hemisphere the compass lags turning through south (roll out early) and leads turning through north (roll out late), by up to about 30° at rate one, with nil error through east and west. Speed changes on east and west headings swing it towards south when you accelerate and north when you slow down (SAND), with nil error heading north or south. Northern-hemisphere books give the opposite. The sizes and the settling here are illustrative.

Try it: in level flight heading 270 near Perth you open the throttle to climb power. What does the compass do?

Press Accelerate west: it swings towards south, so it shows a turn to the left onto a lower heading, although the real heading has not changed. Wait for the speed to settle before reading it.

Try it: using the compass alone, you turn right from 090 onto 180. When do you roll out?

Early, at about 150 on the compass: turning through south it lags by up to about 30°. Press Onto south and play on: once the wings are level the compass catches up to 180.
