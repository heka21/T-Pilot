What to notice. The tab always moves opposite to the elevator. To hold the elevator up for a slower speed, wind on nose-up trim: the tab goes down, the air pushes up on it, and that force at the very back holds the elevator's trailing edge up, so the force on the column disappears. Trim does not fly the aeroplane: attitude first with the elevator, let the speed settle, then trim the pressure away. More power pitches the nose up, so you push, then trim nose-down. The angles and forces are illustrative; the directions are the lesson's.

Try it: trimmed at 90 kt, you slow to 65 kt and hold the new attitude. What do you feel, and which way do you trim?

Press Slowed to 65 kt: you must pull, because the elevator is up and the air is trying to streamline it. Wind on nose-up trim until the force is gone (press Nose-up trim): the tab ends up down and the elevator stays up on its own.

Try it: trimmed at 65 kt, you open the throttle. What happens?

Press Add power: the nose pitches up, so to hold the attitude you now push, and the old trim is too far nose-up. Wind on nose-down trim until the push is gone.
