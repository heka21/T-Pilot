May fly as planned?

What to notice. To fly a particular aeroplane in a particular way, every layer has to say yes: the licence permits the operation, the class or type rating covers the aeroplane, each design feature on it is endorsed, the conditions are within your ratings, the medical allows it (the tighter of licence and medical wins) and you are current. Experience on other features never stands in for a missing endorsement. Figures as the lesson gives them from CASR Part 61 and Part 67; verify the current text.

Try it: Mei has the retractable and constant-speed propeller endorsements. The syndicate's aeroplane also has a tailwheel. May she fly it?

Choose Mei's tailwheel: only the design features layer says no, but one "no" is enough. She needs the tailwheel endorsement first, however experienced she is on the other two features.

Try it: Tom's friends pay for everything and he plans to come home after dark on a Basic Class 2. What two changes make the trip legal?

Choose Tom, as proposed, then Tom, fixed: a four-way equal split of the direct costs, and a return before last light, because the Basic Class 2 keeps him in day VFR whatever his night rating.
