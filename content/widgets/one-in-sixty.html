What to notice. The same offset makes a bigger track error the sooner you find it (you divide by the distance flown) and a bigger closing angle the closer you are to the destination (you divide by the distance to go). Turn by the sum to reach B, then take off only the closing angle once you are back on track.

Try this: 3 nm left after 30 nm, 45 nm to go, heading 085° M. What do you steer?

TE = 3 × 60 ÷ 30 = 6°, CA = 3 × 60 ÷ 45 = 4°. Turn right 10° to 095° M; on regaining track take off the 4° and hold 091° M.

Try this: same fix, but you want to rejoin at a town 15 nm ahead. What changes?

Only the closing angle: 3 × 60 ÷ 15 = 12°. Turn right 6 + 12 = 18° to 103° M; over the town take off 12° and hold 091° M, the same holding heading, because the wind has not changed.
