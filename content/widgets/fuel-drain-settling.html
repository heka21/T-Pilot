What to notice. Straight after refuelling a tank with water in it gives exactly the same clean blue sample as a dry tank: the water is still in suspension. Water is heavier than fuel and sinks to the lowest point, the drain, so allow the fuel to settle first, then drain each point until the sample is clean. The settling time here is illustrative: the lesson gives no figure, so follow the POH.

Try it: you drain a sample a minute after the bowser leaves and it is perfect blue. Can you tick the fuel check?

No. Choose Straight after refuelling, then untick Water in the fuel: the sample looks identical either way, so it proves nothing. Wait for the fuel to settle, then drain again.

Try it: the settled sample shows clear globules at the bottom. What now?

Keep draining (play from Drain until clean) until the sample is clean blue fuel with no water. Then check the colour is right for AVGAS 100LL and drain every other drain point the same way.
