Drinks still in you
over 0.02
8 hours bottle to throttle

What to notice. Two clocks run at once: the law's 8 hours and the liver's roughly one standard drink an hour, and you fly by the later one. After more than about eight drinks the liver is the later one. Then the hangover outlasts both: 24 hours or more after heavy drinking. Real blood alcohol depends on your size and food, so the line counts drinks, not a reading; the hangover's length here is illustrative except for that 24 hours, and the lesson does not define "heavy": taking it as more than eight drinks is this widget's choice.

Try it: ten drinks at a Swan Valley wedding, the last at midnight. Can you make a 0900 lesson?

Press Wedding: at 0900 the 8 hours (0800) have passed, but about one drink is still in you; the alcohol is about gone at 1000. No, and after that many drinks the hangover means rebooking for the next day.

Try it: does a strong coffee and a run get you clear sooner?

Tick it: the line does not move. The liver works at a fixed pace; coffee only makes you feel more awake while the alcohol is still there.
