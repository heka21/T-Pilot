Magnetic track

What to notice. The scale is the conversion: on a WAC a length in millimetres is the distance in kilometres, on a VNC half that, and ÷ 1.852 turns kilometres into nautical miles. Lay the dividers on the latitude scale and the arithmetic disappears: the minutes they span are the nautical miles. The protractor gives a true track, so apply the variation: west is best, east is least.

Try it: a leg measures 251 mm on a WAC, the protractor reads 067°T and the isogonal shows 1.5° W. Distance and magnetic track?

251 mm = 251 km; ÷ 1.852 = 135.5, about 136 NM (check: 25.1 cm × 5.4 = 135.5). West is best: 067 + 1.5 = 068.5°M. Press 251 mm on a WAC.

Try it: the same 120 mm on a VNC and on a WAC. Why the difference?

The VNC is 1:500,000, so 120 mm is 60 km, about 32 NM; the WAC is 1:1,000,000, so 120 mm is 120 km, about 65 NM. Always check which chart before you convert.
