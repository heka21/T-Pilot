What to notice. The two layers cover each other's weak spot: the shell and seal block the high-pitched hiss but let the low drone through, and ANR cancels the low drone because a slow wave gives the circuit time to stay in step. Lift the seal and both lose. The amounts and the circuit delay are illustrative; which layer handles which noise is the lesson's.

Try it: is ANR better at everything?

Compare Drone, ANR with Hiss, ANR: on the drone the anti-noise is a near-perfect mirror and the eardrum trace is almost flat; on the hiss it is out of step and would add to the noise. The passive shell is what deals with the hiss.

Try it: you wear thick-framed glasses under an ANR headset. What happens?

Press Glasses: the lifted seal lets noise round the cushion at every pitch, more than the circuit can cancel, so the eardrum trace grows. Thin-armed glasses and good cushions keep the seal.
