What to notice. The model tells you nothing you did not know; its value is the order. Choose the outcome you want (a landing at Northam) before you pick actions, and Evaluate turns the result into the next Detect, so DECIDE is a loop, not a list. The figures are the lesson's worked example; how far the RPM dips with carb heat is drawn, not measured.

Try it: carb heat makes it worse at first. Was that the wrong action?

Press Carb heat on, then It clears: the melting ice goes through the engine, so roughness and RPM get worse before they recover. Evaluate after it has had time to work, not in the first few seconds.

Try it: what if carb heat changes nothing?

Press No change: Evaluate says it is probably not carb ice. Go round the loop with the new picture: the outcome you chose, a landing at Northam, still stands, and you keep watching the engine all the way.
