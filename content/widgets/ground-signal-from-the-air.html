What to notice. A symbol that looks huge beside you shrinks to a speck from a search aircraft, and pale lines on pale ground vanish at any size. Make it large and contrasting, next to the aircraft, and know V (require assistance) and X (require medical assistance) by heart. The codes are the ERSA's; how wide the searcher's view is and the size at which a mark is seen are illustrative.

Try it: you have laid a neat V of rocks about three metres across. Will the search aircraft see it?

Press A few metres: on the ground it looks plenty big, but from 1,000 ft it is lost in the scrub. Press Big V: twenty-metre lines of rocks on red sand make a shape nobody can miss.

Try it: when do you lay an arrow, and which way does it point?

Only if you really leave the aircraft, for example to walk to a homestead you can see. It means proceeding in this direction and points the way you have gone, so searchers who find the aircraft know where to look.
