What to notice. Almost everything is said digit by digit, with TREE, FIFE and NIN-er standing out; only an altitude in whole thousands and hundreds gets the words TOU-SAND and HUN-dred, and those words tell the listener it is a height. Times go out in UTC, normally as the minutes only.

Try it: how do you say an altitude of 3,500 ft, and what will the wrong answers in the exam look like?

Set Altitude to 3,500 ft: “tree thousand fife hundred”. The distractors are “three five zero zero” (digit by digit, which is for headings and codes) and “thirty-five hundred” (everyday speech).

Try it: you estimate arriving at 0710 WST. What is that in UTC, and how would you normally say it?

Choose 0710 WST: minus 8 hours is 2310 UTC on the previous day. Normally just the minutes, “one zero”; if the hour is needed, “two tree one zero”.
