What to notice. The dangerous moment is not the jet taxiing steadily at idle but the stationary one with its beacon on: to start moving and to turn it uses breakaway thrust, considerably more than idle, and the turn swings that blast sideways across the apron. Once it is rolling the blast shrinks again. Timings, distances and blast lengths here are illustrative and not to scale.

Try it: which is more dangerous to be behind, a jet taxiing at idle or a stationary one with its beacon on?

Press Breakaway, then Rolling: the stationary jet about to move uses far more thrust than the one already rolling. The exam's trap answer is the taxiing one.

Try it: where should you have waited?

Press Blast sweeps: on the taxiway the turning blast reaches you. Tick Wait well back and to the side: out of the line the tail sweeps through, the blast passes you by.
