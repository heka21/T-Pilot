What to notice. The dose is level times time, but the level is the strong lever: every 3 dB off at the ear doubles the safe time, so a well-sealed headset turns three times the daily guide into a fifth of it on the same flight. The guide is the lessons' 85 dB(A) for 8 hours with 3 dB halving; the protection figure is yours to set from your own headset's rating.

Try it: on a 3-hour flight in a 94 dB cabin, how much does a headset that brings your ear down to 82 dB save?

Press Kalbarri bare: 94 dB gives 1 hour a day, so 3 hours is 300%. Press Kalbarri headset: 82 dB gives 16 hours, so the same flight is about 19%.

Try it: 2 hours at 88 dB in the air, then an hour unprotected at 91 dB in the run-up area. How much of the day's guide is gone?

Set 88 dB and 2 h: 50% (4 hours allowed). Then 91 dB and 1 h: another 50% (2 hours allowed). Together 100%: the whole daily guide, half of it on the ground.
