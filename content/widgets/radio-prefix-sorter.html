Prefix

What to notice. Ask two questions: is it about my own aircraft (MAYDAY for grave and imminent danger, PAN PAN for urgency), or about a hazard to everyone else that has not yet harmed anyone (SECURITE)? The same bird is a SECURITE call while it sits on the threshold and a PAN PAN once it has cracked your windscreen. Hazards to navigation also go to ATS, which can warn others and issue a NOTAM. Routine calls never carry a prefix. Check the pronunciation and current wording in AIP GEN 3.4.

Try it: why is an unlit crane near the approach a SECURITE call and not a PAN PAN?

Choose Unlit crane: it is a hazard to the navigation of other aircraft, but nobody's aircraft is in an urgent condition. PAN PAN is urgency about a particular aircraft.

Try it: tick Quiz me, then work through all ten situations. Which ones are not SECURITE?

Turning base and the gusty-but-within-limits wind (no prefix), the bird strike and the smoke (PAN PAN), and the fire (MAYDAY).
