What to notice. Altitude, elevation and the GAF cloud base all start at the sea; height and the TAF or METAR cloud base start at the ground or the aerodrome. Altitude = elevation + height, so fly inland at a steady altitude and your height shrinks as the ground rises, and at an elevated aerodrome the TAF gives the same cloud base as a smaller number. Heights in the picture are exaggerated.

Try it: the GAF gives a cloud base of 3,500 ft for an aerodrome of elevation 650 ft. What would its TAF say for the same cloud?

3,500 − 650 = 2,850 ft above the aerodrome. Press GAF 3,500 at 650 ft: the circuit there is flown at 650 + 1,000 = 1,650 ft on QNH, about 1,850 ft below the base.

Try it: you fly the circuit at 1,300 ft on QNH at a 300 ft aerodrome, then hold that altitude over the 900 ft ridge. How high are you above it?

1,300 − 900 = 400 ft: press Over the 900 ft ridge. The altimeter has not changed; the ground under you has risen 600 ft.
