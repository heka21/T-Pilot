What to notice. Each primary control rotates the aeroplane about one axis through the centre of gravity: ailerons roll about the longitudinal axis, elevator pitches about the lateral axis, rudder yaws about the normal axis. Turn on adverse yaw and roll: the down-going aileron makes more drag, so the nose swings the wrong way until rudder is used to balance.

Try this: stick back only. Which axis does the aeroplane rotate about, and which control surface moved?

The lateral axis (wing tip to wing tip); the elevator trailing edge moved up, pushing the tail down and the nose up.
