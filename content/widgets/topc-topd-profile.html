What to notice. Height to change ÷ rate gives the time; time × the groundspeed for that phase gives the distance. Subtract the starting height, and use the groundspeed (TAS corrected for the wind component), not the cruise TAS. A headwind on the climb pulls TOPC closer to departure; a tailwind on the descent pushes TOPD further out.

Try it: climb from 1,500 ft to 7,500 ft at 600 ft/min, climb TAS 80 kt into a 10 kt headwind. Where is TOPC?

6,000 ÷ 600 = 10 min; groundspeed 80 − 10 = 70 kt, 1.17 NM/min; 10 × 1.17 = 11.7 NM from departure. Press Climb into a headwind.

Try it: descent from 8,500 ft to 1,500 ft at 500 ft/min, TAS 135 kt into a 15 kt headwind. Where is TOPD?

7,000 ÷ 500 = 14 min; groundspeed 120 kt, 2 NM/min; 14 × 2 = 28 NM before the destination. Press Descent into a headwind.
