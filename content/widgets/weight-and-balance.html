What to notice. Weight × arm = moment; add the moments, divide by the total weight and you have the CG. The forward limit slopes inwards at high weight, so a load that is fine at 900 kg can be out at 1100 kg. Burning fuel moves the point along the dashed line: both the take-off and the landing point must stay inside. Figures are for an example four-seat trainer, not your aeroplane: always use the POH.

Try this: two 85 kg adults in front, 30 kg of baggage, full fuel. Within limits?

Weight 770 + 170 + 30 + 144 = 1114 kg (under 1157). Moment 770 + 159.8 + 72.3 + 175.7 = 1177.8 kg·m, CG = 1.057 m. Forward limit at 1114 kg is about 1.016 m, aft limit 1.20 m: inside.

Try this: add two 90 kg passengers in the rear. What has to change?

Weight becomes 1294 kg, 137 kg over MTOW. You must leave people, baggage or fuel behind: 137 kg of fuel is 190 L, almost the tanks, so the answer is a passenger or two, not fuel alone. Check the CG again after each change.
