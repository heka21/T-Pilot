What to notice. A SARTIME is the time, in UTC, by which you will report your arrival: planned arrival plus a sensible margin, minus 8 hours for UTC. Lodge it through NAIPS or by phone before departure, extend it by radio to the FIS before it expires, and cancel it the moment you land, wherever that is. Nothing happens until it passes; then the phases run uncertainty, alert, distress. The 30-minute margin is the lesson's choice, not a rule; the phases are drawn in order, not to scale, and the headwind delay is illustrative.

Try it: depart Jandakot 0800 WST, 2 hours 50 minutes to Kalbarri, a 30-minute margin. What SARTIME do you lodge?

Choose Kalbarri: $0800 + 2{:}50 = 1050$ WST, plus 30 minutes is 1120 WST, and $1120 - 8{:}00 = 0320$ UTC.

Try it: low cloud makes you land at Geraldton at 1010 WST, safe. Why does the SARTIME still matter?

Choose Diverted to Geraldton: until you cancel or amend it, at 0320 UTC (1120 WST) the system starts the alerting phases for an aircraft it thinks is somewhere between Geraldton and Kalbarri.
