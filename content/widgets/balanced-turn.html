What to notice. Lift tilts with the wings. Its vertical part must still equal weight, so total lift grows: the load factor is $1/\cos\theta$. The horizontal part is what turns the aeroplane, so a steeper bank turns faster and tighter. The ball tells you whether the turn is balanced: slip is ball in, skid is ball out, and the fix is rudder on the side the ball is on.

Try this: at 60° of bank, how much does the stall speed rise, and how does the rate of turn compare with 30°?
