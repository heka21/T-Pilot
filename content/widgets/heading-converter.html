True

What to notice. Working from the chart to the cockpit, true then magnetic then compass: variation or deviation east, magnetic or compass least; west is best. Fly east and only the variation changes, because it belongs to the place; turn and only the deviation changes, because it belongs to the aeroplane. Going back from compass to true, reverse every sign. The map is schematic: Perth about 2°W, Sydney about 12°E.

Try it: the compass reads 095°C, the card says 1°W and the variation is 2°W. What is the true direction?

Going back, west is now subtracted: 095 − 1 = 094°M, then 094 − 2 = 092°T. Press Compass 095 back to true to check; going forward again, 092 + 2 + 1 = 095.

Try it: hold 038°T and drag the aeroplane from Perth to Sydney. What happens to the magnetic heading?

It falls from 040°M at Perth (2°W, best) through 038°M on the 0° isogonal to 026°M near Sydney (12°E, least). Only the variation changes as you fly east: deviation belongs to the aeroplane and its heading, not to the place.
