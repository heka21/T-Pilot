Minutes column

What to notice. A 4-figure time looks like a number but is two numbers side by side: hours, and minutes that only run to 59. Add the columns separately and carry an hour each time the minutes reach 60; working backwards, borrow 60 minutes from the hours. Treating the group as one number gives answers like 1680 that are not times at all.

Try it: depart 1545, flight time 1 hour 35 minutes. When do you land?

Minutes 45 + 35 = 80, which is 1 hour 20: write 20, carry 1. Hours 15 + 1 + 1 = 17. Landing 1720, not 1680. Press 1545 + 1:35.

Try it: the Kalbarri runway closes at 0900 WST and the flight takes 3 hours. What is the latest departure, and what is it in UTC?

0900 − 3:00 = 0600 WST. That is before 0800, so in UTC it is the previous day: 2200 UTC. Press Beat the works at 0900; the lesson's plan departs 0540 to land at 0840 with 20 minutes in hand.
