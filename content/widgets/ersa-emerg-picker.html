What to notice. Identify the problem and the airspace first, then read the action: the same radio failure means "land at the nearest suitable aerodrome" in Class G but "follow the last clearance" in controlled airspace. Three codes do the talking: 7700 emergency (and when intercepted, unless ATS says otherwise), 7600 radio failure, 7500 unlawful interference. Calls go on the frequency in use first, then 121.5 MHz. ERSA's section order varies between editions: check your current one.

Try it: VFR at 4,500 ft between Jandakot and Bunbury, the radio goes silent. What does ERSA EMERG tell you to do?

Choose Radio failure, Class G: continue, remain in VMC, land at the nearest suitable aerodrome, broadcast blind and squawk 7600, so any radar that sees you knows the problem without a call.

Try it: the smoke from behind the panel turns into flames. What changes?

Choose Fire: the call is upgraded from PAN PAN to MAYDAY, and the transponder goes to 7700.
