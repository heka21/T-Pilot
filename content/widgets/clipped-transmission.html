What to notice. Only the words between the transmitter reaching power and the release get out, and the words at the edges are the ones that matter: the call-sign first, the key words and your call-sign last. Press the PTT, pause about half a second, speak, finish, then release. The timings and speaking pace here are illustrative, and the clock runs slowed down.

Try it: you pause only 0.3 s after pressing. Does the readback still get through?

Set Speaking starts to 0.30 s after the press: the transmitter is still coming up to power for the first 0.2 s, so the tower hears “…ld short runway two four, Alpha Bravo Charlie”. The first word is damaged; start a little earlier and “hold short” is gone altogether. That is why the pause is about half a second.

Try it: you are expecting traffic information and hear “…harlie, traffic, Piper, 2 o'clock, 5 miles, same level”. Why not just reply as if it were yours?

Choose “…harlie, traffic”: the clipped call-sign fits Alpha Bravo Charlie and the Kilo Lima Charlie also on frequency. Never assume a clipped call was for you, even when you expected it. Reply “Station calling Alpha Bravo Charlie, say again”.
