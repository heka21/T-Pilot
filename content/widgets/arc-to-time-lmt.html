Day VFR: on the ground by

What to notice. The earth turns 15° an hour, so each degree of longitude is 4 minutes and each minute of arc 4 seconds. East of Greenwich the sun comes round earlier, so LMT is ahead of UTC: subtract the arc to time, write the UTC line down and watch midnight, then add 8 hours. WST is simply the sun's time at 120° E, which is why Perth's LMT runs about 16 minutes behind it and Kalgoorlie's about 6 ahead.

Try it: the end-of-daylight graph gives 1820 LMT at Bunbury (115° 40′ E). By when must a day VFR flight be on the ground?

Arc to time 7 h 42 min 40 s ≈ 7 h 43 min. 1820 − 0743 = 1037 UTC; + 8 h = 1837 WST; on the ground by 1827 WST. Cross-check: Bunbury is 4° 20′ west of 120° E, about 17 minutes, and 1820 + 17 = 1837. Press Bunbury last light.

Try it: why does first light at Kalgoorlie fall on the previous day in UTC?

0535 LMT − 8 h 06 min goes back past midnight: 2935 − 0806 = 2129 UTC the day before. Adding 8 hours gives 2929, which is 0529 WST, back on the day you started. Press Kalgoorlie first light.
