What to notice. The vertical part of lift never changes: it always equals weight. Banking tilts lift, so total lift must grow to keep that vertical part. Load factor $n = 1/\cos\theta$ creeps to 1.41 at 45°, doubles by 60° and reaches the normal-category limit near 75°. Stall speed rises with $\sqrt{n}$.

Try this: set 60° of bank. What are the load factor and the stall speed?

2.0 g, because 1 ÷ cos 60° = 2. Stall speed = 52 × √2 = 52 × 1.41 ≈ 74 kt.

Try this: at what bank does a normal-category aeroplane reach its +3.8 g limit in a level turn?

About 75°: cos θ = 1 ÷ 3.8 = 0.263, θ ≈ 75°. Any gust or pull on top of that risks structural damage.
