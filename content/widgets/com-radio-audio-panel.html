What to notice. The knobs change only the standby window, and you transmit and receive on the active frequency only, so a dialled frequency does nothing until you flip it. On the audio panel one MIC selection decides where every call goes, while any number of receive buttons decide what you hear: hearing the CTAF proves nothing about where your next call will go. The station names and frequencies here are examples; take the real ones from ERSA and the VNC.

Try it: you have dialled the tower frequency and made your call, but the tower does not answer. What do you check first?

Whether the tower frequency is in the active window or still in standby (press Tower in standby). The knobs only change standby, and the radio transmits and receives on the active frequency only, so an untransferred frequency means your call went out on the old one.

Try it: you want to hear the ATIS on COM 2 without losing the tower on COM 1. What do you select?

Leave the MIC selector on COM 1 (the tower) and press the COM 2 receive button so you hear COM 2 as well. Turn COM 2 off again once you have the ATIS, so it does not mask tower calls. You transmit on whichever COM the MIC selector chooses, regardless of which ones you are listening to.
