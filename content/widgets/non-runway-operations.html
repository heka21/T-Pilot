You may land
closed by the dumbbell

What to notice. The default is the restrictive one: unless ERSA positively says operations are not restricted to runways, you land on a runway, however smooth the grass looks. With that statement you may use any part of the landing area inside the cones, lined up into wind, but never the area beyond the gables, the apron or the fence line, and the day's ground signals (a white dumbbell: sealed surfaces only) still override it. The lesson gives no crosswind limit: check the components against your aeroplane's. The aerodrome layout is illustrative.

Try it: a strong wind blows across both runways and ERSA says nothing about operations off them. May you land diagonally into wind?

Choose ERSA silent: no. Silence means runways only. Land on the end with the better crosswind component if it is within your limits, or go elsewhere.

Try it: ERSA allows the whole landing area, but a white dumbbell is out. Where may you land?

Choose Dumbbell: only on the sealed runway, 09/27. The dumbbell overrides the normal freedom to use the grass, usually because it is soft or waterlogged.
