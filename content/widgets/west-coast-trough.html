What to notice. Perth's summer weather turns on which side of the trough it is. Trough off or near the coast: Perth is on its eastern side, the wind is a hot, dry easterly from the interior, the sea breeze is held offshore and it is a 40-degree day. Trough inland: Perth is on its western side, the wind swings south-westerly, the Doctor arrives, and storms and dust break out inland along the trough line. Treat its passage like a weak front.

Try it: the trough moves inland during the morning. What happens at Jandakot, and what do you expect inland?

Drag the trough east past Perth: the hot easterly gives way to a south-westerly, the sea breeze comes in and the temperature drops, with shear and turbulence as the change passes. Inland, the convergence along the trough line triggers high-based storms with dust and gusty outflow.
