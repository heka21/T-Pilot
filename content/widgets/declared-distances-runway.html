Declared distances, this direction

What to notice. Each chart figure has its own declared distance: the take-off distance to 50 ft goes against the TODA, which a clearway lengthens because you may climb over it; the landing distance from 50 ft goes against the LDA, which starts at the threshold. A displaced threshold shortens only the LDA, and only for landings over it: the arrowed pavement still counts for the take-off run and for the roll-out the other way. A stopway lengthens only the ASDA, the room to stop after an abandoned take-off.

Try it: a 700 m strip, chart landing distance 520 m. A displaced threshold leaves an LDA of 500 m. Can you land?

Press 700 m strip: 180 m spare. Now Displaced: LDA 500 m: 520 m is 20 m more than the LDA, so no, even though the pavement is still 700 m long. The take-off is unchanged: TODR 640 m against TODA 700 m, 60 m spare (POPA 2.1).

Try it: the northern threshold is displaced 220 m for power poles. Which landings does it cost you?

Press Power poles, landing south: landing over the poles you touch down past the bar, LDA 1,250 − 220 = 1,030 m, still enough for 540 m; the take-off run keeps the full 1,250 m. Press Landing north: you touch down at the southern threshold and may roll out onto the arrowed section, so the LDA is the full 1,250 m (PFRA 2.5).
