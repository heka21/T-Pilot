Window in Perth time

What to notice. The hours are the easy part; the date is the trap. A WST time before 0800 is still the previous day in UTC (the amber end of the UTC tape), and a UTC time from 1600 onwards is already tomorrow in Perth. WA stays on UTC + 8 all year. The eastern row is there for contrast: on standard time the east is 2 hours ahead of Perth, and with summer time in NSW, Victoria, the ACT and Tasmania it is 3 (PNVC 2.2; verify the current arrangements in AIP GEN 2.1). Exam conversions are to standard time unless the question says otherwise.

Try it: a NOTAM closes a runway from 1700 UTC on Thursday. When does it start in Perth?

Set the Perth date to a Friday and drag until the UTC pill reads 1700: the marker sits at 0100 WST, and the UTC readout says the day before. 0100 WST on Friday. Any UTC time from 1600 onward is the next day in Perth.

Try it: Kalbarri works. You take off at 0540 WST and land at 0840. Which UTC dates go on the flight notification, and do you beat the closure?

Take-off is 2140 UTC on Friday (before 0800 WST, so the day before); drag to 0840 and the landing is 0040 UTC on Saturday. The closure, 0100–0300 UTC, is 0900–1100 WST: you land 20 minutes before it starts.
