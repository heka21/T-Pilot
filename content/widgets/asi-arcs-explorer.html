What to notice. Each arc is a permission. White: flap may be out (VS0 to VFE). Green: any air, clean (VS1 to VNO). Yellow: smooth air only, with caution. Red: never. VA, VFO and any second VFE are not on the dial, so they live in your head and the POH. The air load on extended flap grows with the square of speed, so a little over VFE is a lot over the design load. Example speeds for a light trainer, not your aeroplane.

Try it: rushed on downwind, you select full flap at 112 kt with a full-flap VFE of 95 kt. How much over the design load is the flap?

Press Full flap at 112 kt: 112 ÷ 95 = 1.18, and 1.18² = 1.39, so the flap carries about 139% of its design load, 39% over.

Try it: at 100 kt with the first stage out (VFO 85, VFE 105 and 95), are you within limits, and may you select full flap?

Press First stage at 100 kt: you may fly with that flap (100 is below 105) but may not move it (100 is above VFO 85), and full flap would be over its 95 kt VFE anyway. Slow to 85 kt first.
