What to notice. The paper stays the same size: halve the number after the colon and the country on it shrinks to a quarter while every feature grows, so the VTC shows the most detail and the least area. The WAC carries the ground but no airspace; the VNC and VTC carry both; the ERC-L carries the airspace with no ground. The area, the CTR and steps, the R area and the isogonal value are illustrative, not a real chart.

Try it: you will route east past the aerodrome. Which charts show you the CTR and the R area beside your track?

Press WAC: the aerodrome, railway and highway are there, but no CTR, no steps and no R area. Press VNC or VTC and they appear with their limits; ERC-L shows them too, without the ground. Then look up the R area's hours in ERSA.

Try it: on a 1:500,000 VNC, how far on the ground is 4 cm?

One centimetre is 5 km, so 4 cm is 20 km. Press VNC and compare the 10 km bar: it is 2 cm long, twice the WAC's 1 cm.
