What to notice. The canals feel only the start and stop of a rotation: a steady turn fades to "level" within 10 to 20 seconds, and the roll-out then feels like a turn the other way. Trust that feeling and you roll straight back into the bank: the graveyard spiral. The 20° bank and the exact shape of the fade are illustrative; the direction of every false sensation is not.

Try it: in cloud you hold a steady right turn for half a minute. What do you feel, and what happens when you roll out?

Press Steady turn: the cupula is upright and you feel level although you are banked 20°. Press Roll out: wings level, but the fluid runs on and you feel a turn to the left. Hold the attitude indicator level and the feeling fades.

Try it: why does pulling back make a graveyard spiral worse?

Press Spiral: the pilot has rolled back into the bank to stop the false left turn, and now feels level while the nose drops. With the wings banked, back pressure tightens the turn instead of raising the nose. Roll wings level on the attitude indicator and accept the false sensation that follows.
