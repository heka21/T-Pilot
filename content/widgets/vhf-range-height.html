What to notice. VHF travels in straight lines, so it reaches only as far as each antenna's radio horizon, about 1.23 × √height NM, and the two horizons add. Range grows with the square root of height: four times the height only doubles it. Terrain, transmitter power and the airframe shielding the antenna often make the real range less, and a VOR is only reliable within its rated coverage.
