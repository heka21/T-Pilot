What to notice. The chart shows height three ways, and each answers a different question: the tints say where it is high, the spacing of the contours says how steep and which way, and the spot height says exactly how high. Lift the rings and the crowded side becomes the cliff and the spread side the gentle slope. The hill and its heights are illustrative.

Try it: you plan to cross this hill from the west at 2,000 ft on QNH. Which figure do you check, and is it enough?

The spot height, 2180 ft above sea level, the same datum as your QNH altimeter. 2,000 ft is below the top, so you would fly into the hill: climb, or route round it. On a VNC or VTC the maximum elevation figure for the grid square gives the same warning with a margin built in.

Try it: turn the tints off. Can you still tell which face is steep?

Yes: press Contours only. The rings crowd together on the east side, so that face climbs 1,500 ft in a short distance; on the west they are far apart, a gentle slope.
