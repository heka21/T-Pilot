What to notice. Every limit is a fence, and you are bound by the tightest one, whoever set it: a Basic Class 2 would allow 8,618 kg and 5 passengers, but the RPL still stops you at 1,500 kg, and a RAMPC holder may take 1 passenger however many seats there are. Endorsements move only some fences (distance, controlled aerodromes, controlled airspace, height); single-engine, day VFR, private and 1,500 kg belong to the licence itself. Check them in the lesson's order (aircraft, conditions, distance, airspace, recency, medical) and the one you skip is the one that catches you. Limits from CASR Part 61 and Part 67 as the lesson gives them; the presets' distances and the twin's weight are illustrative.

Try it: Sam is flying to York with a friend. What happens if the planned route clips controlled airspace?

Press Sam to York, then tick “Route enters controlled airspace”. The Class G gate fails: Sam holds the controlled aerodrome endorsement but not the controlled airspace one, and the aerodrome endorsement does not cover the airspace en route. Plan a route that stays in Class G and the flight is legal.

Try it: Jo has a RAMPC and wants to take a partner and their teenage son to Northam. What could Jo change to take both?

Press Jo, RAMPC: the passenger fence stops at 1, so the second dot is red. Switch the medical to Basic Class 2 (up to 5 passengers) or Class 2 and the flight passes; the 1,500 kg fence is the licence's either way.
