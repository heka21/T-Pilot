What to notice. Each step covers a different half: listening pays off if the receiver works, transmitting blind if the transmitter does, and 7600 tells radar either way while the transponder has power. You cannot tell which half has gone, so you do all three, then remain in VMC, land at the most suitable aerodrome and telephone ATS. A radio failure on its own is 7600, not 7700.

Try it: after the checks you can hear the Jandakot ATIS clearly but the tower never answers you. Which half is probably working, and does it change what you do?

The receiver is probably working, so the transmitter (or microphone) is the suspect. You still squawk 7600 and still transmit blind, because you cannot be sure the transmitter is dead, and you listen closely because any instruction ATC gives you will reach you.
