Active hours in Perth time
Your plan

What to notice. ERSA gives the hours in UTC, so add 8 hours to see them on your watch; a UTC period that crosses midnight often sits inside one WST morning, and one that does not can run overnight in Perth. An area activated by NOTAM is inactive unless a NOTAM wakes it. Whether active means "ask", "avoid" or "never" comes from the letter and the RA status. HJ, HN and HO (daylight, night, hours of operation) work the same way without the arithmetic. The areas here are illustrations of the format, not real ones; always check the NOTAMs on the day.

Try it: an area is listed active 2200–0200 UTC. You will pass it at 0930 WST. Is it active?

2200 + 8 = 3000, minus 24 is 0600 WST; 0200 + 8 = 1000 WST. Active 0600 to 1000 WST, so at 0930 it is active. Press 2200–0200Z at 0930, then drag your time past 1000 and watch it clear.

Try it: an area is listed active 1400–2200 UTC. Is it active at 0500 WST?

1400 + 8 = 2200 WST; 2200 + 8 = 3000, which is 0600 WST the next day. Active 2200 to 0600 WST, overnight in Perth, so 0500 is inside it: active. Press 1400–2200Z at 0500.
