What to notice. Enter in the same order every time: pressure height and temperature, then TODA, wind component, slope and surface. In each correction panel the line runs parallel to the guide lines from the reference line to your input; the climb weight limit uses the first panel only. More runway and a headwind raise the weight; heat, height, tailwind, upslope and grass lower it. This chart and its weights are illustrative, built only to show the method: real answers come from the workbook chart or your POH.

Try it: work the Northam inputs (1,330 ft, 33°, TODA 1,100 m, 13 kt headwind, 1% up, grass). Then tick the error box. What changes?

The line no longer moves in the TODA, wind, slope and surface panels, so it lands on a different weight with nothing to warn you. In each sloping panel follow the guide lines to the input before carrying straight across.

Try it: compare Cool, sealed, into wind with Hot, high, tailwind. Does the answer move the way it should?

Yes: the hot, high, tailwind, upslope, grass take-off gives a much lower weight. If a chart answer ever moves the other way, a line went the wrong way in one of the panels.
