What to notice. Turn the chart so the track line points the way you are going. Then every feature sits on the same side of the line as it does outside the windscreen, and you can read from map to ground without turning anything in your head. Upside-down printing on a southbound leg does not matter; the shapes do. Fold the chart to the leg and keep a thumb on the last fix.

Try it: you fly 200°M with the chart north-up and a railway lies to the right of your track line on the chart. Where is it outside?

On your left. Flying roughly south, the page's right is your left. Press 200° track-up: the railway moves to the left of the line, the same side as outside.

Try it: tracking east with the chart north-up, where on the page is something that is on your left?

Towards the top of the page: ahead is to the right of the page and left is up. Press East, north-up, then switch to track-up.
