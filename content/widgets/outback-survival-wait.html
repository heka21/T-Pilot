What to notice. Protection, location, water, food, in that order, and stay with the aircraft. In the shade the 4 litres lasts; walking in the sun it is gone by evening, because the water you lose is sweat. And from the air an aeroplane, a big V and a column of smoke are easy to see; a person on foot in the scrub is not. When the search arrives and how fast the water goes are illustrative.

Try it: why is "walk to the town for help" the wrong answer?

Turn on Walk and play to the end: the water is gone by evening, the night is spent without shelter, and the searchers, following your notified route and the PLB, find the aircraft but not you. Stay with the aircraft unless help is certain and close.

Try it: should you ration the 4 litres to a few mouthfuls a day?

No: compare In the shade with Walking. Dehydration harms you whether the water is lost as sweat or never drunk. Rest in the shade by day, work in the cool, and drink what you need: conserve sweat, not water.
