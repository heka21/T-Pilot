Attention and flying

What to notice. The cause is the eyes and the inner ear telling different stories: head down at a chart in bumpy air is the classic conflict. Eyes on the horizon and taking the controls work on the cause; cool air and smooth flying raise the threshold and cut the motion. Performance falls in the quiet first stages, long before the sick bag. The rates and timings are illustrative; the six stages and the remedies are the lesson's.

Try it: your back-seat passenger is reading her phone through the thermals. What happens, and what is the fix that works on the cause?

Press Chart in thermals and play: quiet, yawning, pale, sweating, nausea. Change the look to the horizon and play again: the eyes see the motion the inner ear feels, and she stays at the early stages at worst.

Try it: why speak up at the first yawn rather than wait?

Press Act early: the remedies go in at stage 2 and the queasiness ebbs before it reaches the sick bag. Without them the same flight ends at stage 6.
