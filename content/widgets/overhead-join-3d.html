What to notice. The join never descends through the circuit. You cross the aerodrome above circuit height, lose height in a turn on the dead side (the side opposite the circuit), cross the upwind end at circuit height heading in the crosswind direction, and join crosswind, then downwind with everyone else. Change the runway or the circuit direction and the dead side moves with the pattern. The lesson gives no figure for the overhead height; the model crosses 500 ft above a 1,000 ft circuit.

Try this: switch to a right-hand circuit for runway 24. Which side does the descent now happen on, looking along the landing direction?

On the left. The dead side is always the side opposite the circuit, so with right-hand turns it moves to the left of the runway. Build it from the landing direction and the published circuit direction every time, never from memory of "the right side".
