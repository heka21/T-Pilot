What to notice. The runway holding position works one way: the solid lines face you on the way to the runway and mean stop; the dashed lines face the runway, so after landing you cross them freely. Yellow paint is taxiway, white is runway; red signs are mandatory, yellow signs only inform. The layout and timings are illustrative.

Try it: rolling out after landing, the first lines you meet across the taxiway are two dashed ones. Stop or go?

Go. Tick Vacating and play: the dashed side faces the runway, so you cross without stopping, and you keep going until the whole aeroplane is past the solid lines, where you are clear of aircraft using the runway.

Try it: which sign told you to stop, and which only told you where to go?

The red sign with white letters, 24R, at the holding position is mandatory: you do not pass it without permission. The black-on-yellow direction sign (24R straight ahead, B to the left) is information only.
