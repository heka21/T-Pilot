What to notice. The surface rises 1 ft for every 20 ft out, so close to the strip it is very low: only about 25 ft at 150 m, about 50 ft at 300 m. Anything poking through it fails the strip, however long the runway is. Drag the obstacle or use the sliders. The 1 in 20 figure is the lesson's from AC 91-02: verify it against the current AC.

Try it: how far beyond the end of the strip must a 50 ft tree be?

50 × 20 = 1,000 ft, about 305 m. Press 50 ft tree at 300 m: at 300 m the surface is about 49 ft, so the tree is level with it; a few metres further out it is below.

Try it: the farm strip has trees about 300 m beyond its northern end. What is the most they can be?

About 50 ft. Press Tall gums at 300 m: a 70 ft gum breaks the surface by about 21 ft, so plan to depart the other way, into wind if you can.
