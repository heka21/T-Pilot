What to notice. Almost every "dead radio" here is a plug, a switch, a setting or a breaker, and working along the chain in order finds it. The squelch test halves the search: hiss but no stations puts the fault in front of the radio, no hiss puts it between the radio and your ear. Reset a breaker once only, and fly first: one check, look out, the next check.

Try it: on "Tower silent", why does flipping the frequency not fix it?

There are two faults. After the flip you still hear nothing, and opening the squelch gives no hiss, which points behind the radio: the audio panel is set correctly, so check the headset, where the headphone plug has worked out of its socket. Push it home and the tower comes back.

Try it: on "Total silence", the breaker pops again after one reset. What now?

Leave it out: a second trip means a real fault and a fire risk. Treat the radio as failed: squawk 7600 if you were talking to ATC, follow the ERSA EMERG radio failure procedure, transmit blind, stay in VMC, land at the most suitable aerodrome, report arrival, and write it on the maintenance release.
