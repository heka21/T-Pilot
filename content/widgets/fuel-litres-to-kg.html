Volume
Weight for the weight and balance

What to notice. For avgas the kilograms are always about three-quarters of the litres. Multiply litres by the SG to get kilograms; divide kilograms by the SG to get litres. If your kilograms come out bigger than your litres, you went the wrong way.

Try this: 120 L of avgas on board, SG 0.72. What goes in the weight and balance?

120 × 0.72 = 86.4 kg. Writing "120" in the kilograms column would overstate the fuel by about 34 kg.

Try this: the weight and balance allows only 90 kg of fuel. How many litres do you ask for?

90 ÷ 0.72 = 125 L. Litres are the bigger number for avgas, because a litre weighs less than a kilogram.
