What to notice. Each load pushes down at its own arm, and the balance point is the total moment divided by the total weight. Loads far aft (the rear seat at 2,900 mm, the locker at 3,500 mm) pull it aft out of proportion to their weight. Taking a load out changes the divisor as well as the moment, so re-calculate after every change. Drag the bag between the front seats, the rear seat and the locker. The aeroplane and its figures are the lesson's worked example.

Try it: four on board is aft of the limit. Does moving the bag forward or taking it out fix it?

Press Four on board: 1,038 kg, CG 2,154 mm, 34 mm aft. Bag forward: 2,125 mm. Bag out: 2,127 mm, further aft than moving it, because the divisor shrank too. Neither is enough. Rear seat empty: 968 kg and 2,100 mm, inside the limits.

Try it: solo with a 30 kg bag and 120 L. Inside at take-off? And after burning 60 L?

Press Solo take-off: 881.4 kg and 2,119 mm, inside by only 1 mm. Press Solo, 60 L burned: 838.2 kg and 2,110 mm. The tanks at 2,300 mm are aft of the CG, so burning fuel moves the CG forward.
