What to notice. More power, more hazard; more distance, less, but only gradually, because the stream widens and slows as it goes. The push on your aeroplane goes with the stream speed squared, so 50% faster is more than twice the push and twice as fast is four times. Off to the side of the tail the stream is far weaker. The speeds, distances and shapes are an illustrative model built on the lesson's worked example; check the current AIP for any specified distances.

Try it: the stream reaching you rises from 20 kt to 30 kt. How much harder does it push?

Press Taxiing: 20 kt, then More power: 30 kt: (30 ÷ 20)² = 2.25 times. Press Closer: 40 kt: (40 ÷ 20)² = 4 times. Your tail and wings feel the push, not the speed.

Try it: where do you wait at the holding point behind it?

Press Take-off power and drag your aeroplane: directly behind the tail the stream is strong even four lengths back. Press To the side: off the line of its tail, and as far back as the taxiway allows, the stream is a fraction of that.
