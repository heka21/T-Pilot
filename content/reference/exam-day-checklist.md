## A week before

- ☐ **Booking confirmed**: date, time and venue, and you know how to get there. Fee: RPLA $189.53, PPLA $217.24.
- ☐ **One list only**: decide Airservices or Jeppesen and pack only that list ([permitted materials](/reference/permitted-materials)).
- ☐ **Documents current** for the exam date (AIP amendments, ERSA, Part 91 MOS compilation). Replace anything superseded.
- ☐ **Self-printed documents** are printed straight from the website on A4 or smaller, with headers and footers, **bound** (no loose sheets), each in a clearly marked folder with nothing else inside.
- ☐ **No notations** anywhere. Underlining and highlighting are fine.
- ☐ **Tags within limits**: AIP Book (or Jeppesen equivalent) 15 max, ERSA 5 max, everything else none. Tags are English words only: no notes, formulae or cross-references.
- ☐ **Not packing** home-made indexes or contents pages for the AIP or regulations.
- ☐ **PPLA**: Sydney, Townsville and Bourke WACs and AC 91-02 are packed.
- ☐ **VFRG**: if you take it, also take the current regulations (the VFRG may be out of date).

## Equipment

- ☐ **Basic calculator**: +, -, x, ÷, √, single memory (%, GST keys optional). No HMS key, no sin/cos/tan, not programmable, not an aviation computer. A spare battery or a solar model.
- ☐ **Nav equipment**: one manual flight computer (for example a CR-type), protractor, dividers, compass, ruler or straight-edge.
- ☐ No need to bring pens, pencils, eraser, ruler or scribble paper: the invigilator provides them.

## On the day

- ☐ **Photo ID** that CASA accepts (check "Acceptable photo ID for exams" on the CASA site).
- ☐ **Arrive early**: time to settle, check in and have your materials checked.
- ☐ **Phone and smartwatch off and left outside** the exam room. No electronic devices of any kind.
- ☐ Remember: everything handed out, including the workbook and your scribble paper, goes back to the invigilator.

## In the exam

- **Budget time**: 2 h for RPLA, 3.5 h for PPLA. Do the quick questions first, flag the long calculations and come back.
- **Whole-number answers**: work carefully and round only at the end. CASA specifically recommends not rounding at intermediate stages of fuel calculations.
- **Fuel policy**: reserves per CASR Part 91 MOS Chapter 19, table 19.02(2) item 1 (aeroplane under 5,700 kg, VFR by day). AVGAS specific gravity 0.72 kg/L in the workbook.
- **Read the question twice**: units (kg/lb, L/kg, ft/m), which loading system or chart, and whether it asks for take-off or landing.
- Pass mark is 70%. Don't leave anything blank.

## Study tips tied to CASA's weak areas

CASA publishes the topics candidates most often get wrong ([exam formats](/reference/exam-formats)).

**RPLA**

- **Glide speeds and L/D ratio**: best glide is at max L/D; know how weight and wind change glide range and speed. See [RBKA 3.4](/notes/RBKA/3.4) and [RBKA 3.1](/notes/RBKA/3.1).
- **Wind shear**: learn the effect of an increasing vs decreasing headwind on IAS and flight path. See [RBKA 3.7](/notes/RBKA/3.7).
- **Forecasts**: practise decoding TAF, METAR and GAF and checking alternate requirements. See [RMTC 2.2](/notes/RMTC/2.2).
- **Local winds**: sea and land breezes, anabatic and katabatic flows: when and why. See [RMTC 2.1](/notes/RMTC/2.1).
- **Take-off and landing charts**: work the [workbook charts](/reference/workbook) (Figs 3 to 6) until each takes under three minutes.

**PPLA**

- **Take-off and landing charts**: as above, plus slope, surface and crosswind corrections. See [POPA 2.2](/notes/POPA/2.2).
- **Time/distance and revised estimates**: do a whole nav log by hand, then revise ETAs mid-leg. See [PNVC 2.4](/notes/PNVC/2.4).
- **Density and pressure height**: practise from QNH and temperature with ISA deviation. See [POPA 2.3](/notes/POPA/2.3).
- **Loading systems ALPHA, BRAVO and CHARLIE**: work each [loading system](/reference/workbook) end to end, including fuel burn shifting the CG. See [POPC 2.1](/notes/POPC/2.1).
- **Forecasts**: as for RPLA, plus area forecasts and en route planning. See [PMTC 2.7](/notes/PMTC/2.7).
