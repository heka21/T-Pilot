The **RPL, PPL & CPL (Aeroplane) Workbook, Version 3.0a (2 December 2021)** is the booklet of charts and
loading systems that the invigilator gives you in the RPLA and PPLA exams. Exam questions that say
"refer to Fig 5" or "use loading system BRAVO" mean this workbook. A copy is bundled with the app:

**[Open the workbook (PDF)](/static/rpl-ppl-cpl-aeroplane-workbook.pdf)**

The workbook says it is for training for the CASA RPL, PPL and CPL examinations only. The copy used in the
exam must stay in the exam room and is handed back to the invigilator.

## Changes in version 3

1. Cross-wind limits added to the take-off and landing charts.
2. Fuel policy changed to reflect CASR Part 91 or Part 135 requirements. For RPLA and PPLA it is CASR Part 91 MOS, Chapter 19, Table 19.02, aeroplane with MTOW < 5,700 kg (piston or turboprop), VFR by day.
3. AVGAS specific gravity of **0.72 kg/litre** for all loading systems.

## How to practise with it

- Print the charts, or keep the PDF open next to the quiz. Questions in this app that use the workbook show the page above the question (folded away in mock exams, like the booklet on your desk); the loading and chart lessons show the real pages too.
- Do each chart question **with a ruler and pencil on paper** like the exam. Reading a chart on screen is easier than on paper.
- For loading systems, write the full table (item, mass, arm or index, moment) every time, even when you think you can skip steps. Most errors are skipped lines or the wrong fuel density.
- Note the cross-wind limits on the take-off and landing charts: questions can hinge on them.
