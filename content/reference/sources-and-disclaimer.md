## Disclaimer

**This app is a study aid only.** All notes, questions and flashcards are **original study material**
written for this app. They are **not CASA exam questions**, not taken from CASA exams, and not CASA
material. Passing the practice quizzes or mock exams here does not mean you will pass the CASA exam.

Regulations change. **Always verify regulatory numbers** (fuel reserves, VMC minima, altitudes,
distances, times, document requirements) against the **current AIP, Part 91 MOS and VFRG** before
relying on them in the exam or in the air. Where anything in this app conflicts with the current
legislation or CASA publications, the official source is correct.

Report anything wrong with the "report a problem" box at the bottom of each page; reports appear under
[Issues](/flags).

## Sources

- **Part 61 Manual of Standards, Schedule 3** (Aeronautical knowledge standards). The syllabus, unit
  codes and knowledge elements in this app are taken verbatim from it (Authorised Version F2021C00449,
  registered 20/05/2021, via the Federal Register of Legislation). Element codes such as `RBKA 3.6.2`
  are the official codes.
- **CASA, Recreational pilot licence (aeroplane) exam (RPLA)** page. Source: casa.gov.au, retrieved
  2026-10-03. Format, fee, units, permitted materials, pass rates, fuel policy and common errors.
- **CASA, Private pilot licence (aeroplane) exam (PPLA)** page. Source: casa.gov.au, retrieved
  2026-10-03. As above for the PPLA.
- **CASA, Materials allowed in exams** page. Source: casa.gov.au, retrieved 2026-10-03. Provided and
  required materials, tagging, self-printed documents, nav equipment and the basic calculator.
- **CASA, RPL, PPL & CPL (Aeroplane) Workbook, Version 3.0a (2 December 2021).** Bundled at
  [/static/rpl-ppl-cpl-aeroplane-workbook.pdf](/static/rpl-ppl-cpl-aeroplane-workbook.pdf) under
  CC BY 4.0. Source: Civil Aviation Safety Authority. See [the workbook page](/reference/workbook).

Each note lists its own references at the bottom of the page.

## What is not a source

The question count in the mock exams (60) and the per-unit weightings used to build them are this
app's own estimates: CASA does not publish them.
