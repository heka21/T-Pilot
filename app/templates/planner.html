How the plan is built

  • Every syllabus subtopic is spread over your study days in syllabus order: the RPLA units first, then the PPLA units. Reading time is estimated from each lesson's length (about 100 words a minute, plus time for diagrams, widgets, worked examples and self-check questions).
  • Each study day starts with the flashcards due, and from the third study day adds a short quiz on recent topics.
  • The last few study days before each exam are revision only: flashcards, weak-areas quizzes and a full mock exam two study days out. There is also a midway mock in each block.
  • If everything doesn't fit, the plan tells you how many extra minutes per session or extra days you need.
  • Ticks happen automatically when you mark a subtopic as studied, finish a quiz or mock, or clear your cards; you can also tick items by hand.
